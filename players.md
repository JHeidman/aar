## Time difference

Finland is normally **7 hours ahead** of Rochester (e.g. 1:00 pm in Rochester = 8:00 pm in Finland).
It's **6 hours** for a couple of weeks each spring and fall, when the US and EU change clocks on different dates:
- **Spring:** from the 2nd Sunday in March (US) to the last Sunday in March (EU).
- **Fall:** from the last Sunday in October (EU) to the 1st Sunday in November (US). In 2026 that's Oct 25 – Nov 1.
