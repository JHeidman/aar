# Rulings & Rules Questions

Questions that came up in play, the answer, and where it came from.

**Source key:** Rulebook = cited rule number (living rules, June 2024) · Scenario = scenario book · BGG = designer/forum answer (link) · House = our agreed interpretation until we find out.

| Date | Question | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-27 | How big must a stack be to exert a ZOC? | At least **3 REs** of assault units. A 1-RE brigade alone has no ZOC. | Rulebook 2.6 |
| 2026-10-27 | Is HQ Reserve Capacity counted in units or REs? | **REs.** Note: the glossary says "number of units", and the 15.1.3 example says "3 units", so the book contradicts itself. Rule 15.1.3 itself says REs; going with that. | Rulebook 15.1.3 (vs. glossary) |
| 2026-10-27 | Crossing Swords: how many supply points? | NATO 40, WP 12. No new SPs during the scenario. | Scenario 1 |
| 2026-11-11 | B4 Bavarian Option: is the pre-GT1 nuclear authorization roll before or after the WP pre-GT1 move? | Order doesn't matter. The roll can be made "once at any moment in a GT"; the scenario just allows it during pre-GT1. Players also roll again in each End Stage (step 9), and retaliation is automatically authorized. | Rulebook 18.2; Scenario B4.2 |

## Open questions

Things we still need to check (rulebook, BGG, or ask the designer).

- **Crossing Swords: does initiative ever change?** The turn sequence includes Initiative Determination, but the variant adds "use the Battles Won mechanic and determine initiative each GT", which suggests the base scenario keeps WP as initiative player all 5 turns. Check BGG.
- **Crossing Swords: who needs which hex?** The victory conditions don't name a side. Reading: the initiative player (WP) must take 0401 and hold 0410, since the variant gives NATO the mirror-image task.

- **B4: air recon count.** The marker track says NATO 3 / WP 6, but the card's reinforcements say 1 recon mission per GT for each side. Likely the track is the game total and 1/GT is the per-turn limit. Agree with Eero.
