# Red Strike — Game Facts

- **Publisher:** VUCA Simulations (2023)
- **Designer:** Yves Rettel
- **Subject:** A hypothetical NATO vs. Warsaw Pact war in summer 1989. Operational land, air and sea warfare in Central Europe and the North Atlantic.
- **Players:** 2–6
- **Awards:** Charles S. Roberts Awards for Best Operational Game, Best Hypothetical Game and Outstanding Presentation.
- **Scenarios:** 20 — 10 exercise, 6 battle, 4 campaign. See `scenarios.md`.
