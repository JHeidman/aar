# Red Strike — Game Facts

- **Publisher:** VUCA Simulations (2023)
- **Designer:** Yves Rettel
- **Subject:** A hypothetical NATO vs. Warsaw Pact war in summer 1989. Operational land, air and sea warfare in Central Europe and the North Atlantic.
- **Players:** 2–6
- **Awards:** Charles S. Roberts Awards for Best Operational Game, Best Hypothetical Game and Outstanding Presentation.
- **Scenarios:** About 12, including small training scenarios. The first is **Crossing Swords**, which teaches movement and combat.

## Things others have noted (unconfirmed — check the rulebook)

From a forum post about Crossing Swords:
- A unit stack needs at least 3 REs (regiment equivalents) to exert a ZOC.
- An HQ's reserve capacity is counted in REs, not number of units.
